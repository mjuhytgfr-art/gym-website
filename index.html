About the club

A BRIGHT, OPEN FLOOR BUILT FOR TRAINING

Fitness Pulse is a gym in Bhomiya Nagar, signed as a Hardcore Health Club. Inside, yellow-and-white walls, big mirrors and rubber-tile flooring set the tone for training.

The floor brings strength machines, dumbbell racks and a row of treadmills together with a Cross Fit functional area and a climbing wall, so you can change up your session without leaving the building.

“Look in the mirror, that’s your competition.”
